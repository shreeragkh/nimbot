This document gathers comprehensive information about Naipunnya Institute of Management & Information Technology (NIMIT) sourced from various online resources. It is intended to train a chatbot to answer queries on topics ranging from the institute’s history, academic programs, policies, faculty, infrastructure, events, placements, and more.

1. Overview & Background
    • Name & Identity:
Naipunnya Institute of Management & Information Technology (NIMIT) is managed by the Archdiocese of Ernakulam–Angamaly.
Motto: “To reach the unreachable.”
    • History:
        ◦ Established in 1998 as a Vocational Training Centre.
        ◦ Formally inaugurated in 2002 as an affiliated college of the University of Calicut.
        ◦ Recognized as a Centre of Excellence with sister concerns including Naipunnya School of Management, Naipunnya Business School (NBS), and Naipunnya Public Schools.
    • Accreditations & Certifications:
        ◦ NAAC Accredited with a B++ grade.
        ◦ ISO 9001–2015 certified.
        ◦ Known as one of the premier professional training institutes in South India.
    • Location & Contact:
        ◦ Located in Pongam, Koratty, Thrissur, Kerala (PIN 680308).
        ◦ Contact details (phone and email) are available on the official website.
